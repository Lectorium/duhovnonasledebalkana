The Vision of Isaiah

The Vision (chapters 6–11 of the Ascension of Isaiah) belongs to ancient Christian apocryphal literature. It describes the prophet’s heavenly ascent and Christ’s descent. The Slavonic and second Latin versions transmit this section separately.

Medieval Bogomil and Cathar reception is significant for textual circulation. Specifically Bogomil redaction remains debated: reception does not establish Bogomil authorship.

Witnesses: Moscow, State Historical Museum, 1063/1604, ff. 90r–95r (12th–13th century); Zagreb, Croatian Academy, III.22, ff. 257–260v (14th century). The second Latin version was printed in Venice in 1522. This is a bibliographic overview, not a translation.
