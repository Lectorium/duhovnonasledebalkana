Hval’s Miscellany (1404)

Preserved in Bologna (BUB, Ms. 3575B), the codex contains 359 numbered parchment folios, including the New Testament, Psalter, canticles and apocrypha, in Bosnian Cyrillic. In the colophon (f. 359r), Hval identifies himself as a Christian and names patron Hrvoje Vukčić Hrvatinić and Bosnian Church leader Radomir.

Its illuminations combine Eastern and Western elements. Association with the Bosnian Church alone does not establish dualist theology: the contents require separate analysis. Based on institutional catalogues, not direct manuscript collation.
