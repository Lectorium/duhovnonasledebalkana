The Council of Žiča (1220–1221)

Domentijan and Teodosije

Domentijan describes heretics persuaded through Sava’s preaching. The later author Teodosije distinguishes unbaptized dissenters from Latin Christians, for whom he describes anointing. These differences also reflect changing ecclesiastical attitudes towards Rome.

Persuasion and coercion

According to Roach, reconversion involved Slavonic preaching and incentives for nobles; persistent dissenters still faced expulsion. This was not religious freedom in the modern sense. Hagiographical narratives are not council minutes.
