Interrogatio Iohannis — The Secret Supper

Manuscript tradition

The two Latin recensions are represented by the Vienna codex (ÖNB, lat. 1137, ff. 158v–160r) and the Carcassonne tradition (Dole, ms. 109, ff. 44–46). A note in the latter associates the text with Nazario of Concorezzo and Bulgaria. Neither the original nor a medieval Serbian witness of this apocryphon is available.

Doctrine and commentary

The dialogue between John and Christ presents a dualist cosmology, from Satan’s rebellion to the Last Judgment. It is significant for comparative Balkan and Western European history. The original language and transmission remain research questions. This entry introduces the text rather than providing a complete translation.
