The Ritual of Radoslav

Vatican Library manuscript Borg. ill. 12, consisting of 60 paper folios, was copied by Radosav for Gojsav during the reign of Stephen Tomaš (1443–1461). The colophon on f. 58v also names did Ratko. The ritual on ff. 56–58 parallels the Lyon Cathar ritual. Hamilton and Stoyanov discuss both possible Bogomil ancestry and Western transmission.

The colophon offers a rare personal witness: the scribe asks readers not to mock his errors, recalls the toil of his hands and invokes a blessing. This paraphrases the colophon published in Marinka Šimić’s review of Anica Nazor’s 2008 edition; it is not a manuscript transcription.
