Faith, Dissent, and Conscience

A story of faith, dissent, and conscience

The spiritual history of the Balkans, with particular attention to Serbia, Bogomilism, and freedom of conscience.

Duhovno nasleđe Balkana is an independent cultural project devoted to the history of religious ideas and the relations between conscience, institutions, and power in the Balkan region. It begins with medieval testimonies about Bogomilism and broadens to Serbia, Bosnia, and the spiritual networks of south-eastern Europe. It then turns to contemporary memory, without seeking a direct descent from medieval dissidents to modern Serbia.

The guiding question

The project places different periods in dialogue through a question: how can the dignity of conscience be defended when a political or religious authority demands obedience? This is an explicitly philosophical comparison; it does not argue for historical continuity between medieval Bogomilism and contemporary Serbian society.

The research is independent and not affiliated with a university or institution.

Curator, publications, and contact

The project is curated by Simone Pozzi and is independent of universities and institutions. Personal publications are presented separately from the site’s historical contributions.
